Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

Ask a Cryptid is a small, slightly silly project, but we still take your privacy seriously. Here's what we collect, why, and what we do (and don't do) with it — in plain language.

What we collect

There are two places where we collect information:

  • The contact form. If you reach out or suggest a cryptid, we collect whatever you type in — your message, and your name and email if you choose to include them. We use this only to read your message and reply if needed.
  • Questions you ask the cryptids. When you ask a cryptid something, the text of your question is sent to Anthropic's API (the AI service that gives Bigfoot, Mothman, and friends their questionable personalities) so it can generate a response. We don't attach your name or any account to these questions — they're just the text needed to get an answer.

How payment works

If you upgrade to unlock the full roster of cryptids, your payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see, touch, or store your full card number or other payment details — Stripe takes care of that, and we only receive confirmation that a payment went through.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to get a general sense of how many people visit the site and what's popular. It's built to be privacy-friendly — it doesn't use cookies and doesn't build a personal profile of individual visitors, just aggregated, anonymized traffic trends.

We don't sell your data

We're not in the business of selling or renting your information to anyone, ever. Everything described above is used only to run and improve this site.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, reach out through our contact form and we'll get back to you.

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above reflects the latest version.
